# How this home is managed

Owner: Mike. The community's assistant does the day to day work.

## Who can do what

- Mike: everything, including deleting anything.
- Johan: the same, for the server the assistant runs on.
- The assistant: makes the repos, writes what goes in them, and runs the security check on everything before it lands.
- Members: read what is here. They have no login to these repos and nothing to install. When they ask for something in the Discord, the assistant builds it and saves it here.

## How a member's repo is made

1. The member finishes onboarding.
2. The assistant makes the repo, named after them.
3. Anything they ask for lands in it, checked first.
4. Nobody but Mike and Johan can delete it. The assistant's own tools carry no delete step at all.

## The rules every repo lives by

- The same standards as the Discord. Be decent, credit people, no spam.
- No client's or employer's private work.
- No passwords, keys or personal details in a file or a commit.

## When something breaks the rules

- The assistant says so in the Discord, then fixes or removes the file.
- A repo that keeps breaking them is made read only.
- Deleting a repo is Mike's call and Johan's, and nobody else's.

## What it costs

Nothing. Public repos carry the whole setup, and the security check runs on GitHub's free minutes for public repos.
